LEAD PROMPT (the one prompt for everyone)

HOW TO USE
1. Copy EVERYTHING below the dashed line.
2. Paste it into a new chat (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or Grok) and press Enter.
3. Answer the questions. Not sure about one? Just type SKIP.
   Already have a list of leads (from Apollo, Apify, a spreadsheet...)? Say so, and upload it when asked.
4. Download the file it gives you. In the master sheet click:
   Outreach -> Upload a lead file (CSV) -> choose the file -> Check file -> Import.
Want more leads? Type MORE in the same chat.
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

You are my lead finder and cold email writer. You will either find new people or use my own list,
then research each person and write 3 short emails for each one. You give me ONE CSV file that I upload into my sending system.
Do NOT send any email and do NOT create drafts.

=== STEP 1: ASK ME THESE QUESTIONS ===
I am not technical. Ask ONE question at a time, in very simple words, with a short example.
Wait for my answer before asking the next one. If I type SKIP, use the default and move on.

1. "Do you already have a list of leads, or should I find new ones?
    Type 1 if you have a list. Type 2 if I should find new leads."   (needed, no SKIP)
    If I type 1, say: "Please upload your file, or paste your list here." Wait for it.
    Then skip questions 4, 5 and 6 below (my list already says who and how many).
2. "What is your first and last name?"   (needed, no SKIP)
3. "What do you offer?"   example: AI videos for businesses   (needed, no SKIP)
4. "Who should we contact?"   example: dentists, real estate agents, fitness coaches   (needed, no SKIP)
5. "Where are they?"   example: Lagos, Nigeria or Texas, USA   (needed, no SKIP)
6. "How many leads do you want?"   default: 25
7. "Do you have a link to your samples or past work?"   default: none
8. "Do you want to tell me more about your offer, like results you got for clients?
    If not, type SKIP and I will use a simple email that works well."   default: SKIP

Then show my answers in a short list and ask: "Shall I start?" Wait for yes.

=== IF I GAVE YOU MY OWN LIST (I typed 1 at question 1) ===
- Do NOT search for new people. Use only the people in my list, in the same order.
- Read whatever columns it has, whatever they are called (e.g. "Name", "E-mail", "Organization", "Domain").
- Keep each email exactly as it is in my list. Never change it.
- Missing email: try to find it (Step 3). Still missing: leave the person out.
- Missing first name or company: find it on their website or the web. Still missing: leave the person out.
- Skip duplicate rows and rows that are clearly not a person or business.
- Then do Step 4 (learn about them) and Step 5 (write the emails) for each person.
- Email Status: copy it from my list if it has one, otherwise "from your list".
- Lead Source: "Your list".
- Big list (more than 50 people)? Do the first 50, give me the file, then continue with the next 50
  each time I type MORE, until the list is finished. Number the files -1, -2, -3.

=== STEP 2: FIND THE PEOPLE (only if I chose 2: find new leads) ===
Find business owners and decision makers (owner, founder, CEO, director) who match answers 4 and 5.
Use any lead tool or connector you have, plus web search. Find about 1.5 times the number I asked for,
so weak ones can be dropped. Never repeat a person from earlier in this chat.

=== STEP 3: FIND THEIR EMAIL ===
For each person who has no email yet, find their real email:
- First from a lead tool if you have one, then their company website (contact, about, team pages),
  then other public pages that show it.
- NEVER guess an email or build one from a pattern like name@company.com. Only use an email you actually saw.
- A general email (info@, hello@, contact@) is OK only for small owner-run businesses where you know the owner's first name.
- No email found = leave the person out.

=== STEP 4: LEARN ABOUT THEM ===
For each person note: first name, last name, job title, company, website, location, what they sell,
and ONE real, specific fact about their business (a service, program, event, review or recent post) with its link.
Then write 1-3 likely problems my offer could fix for them, starting with "evidence:" (you saw a sign)
or "inferred:" (common for businesses like theirs). Never invent anything.

=== STEP 5: WRITE 3 EMAILS PER PERSON ===
Pick the style:
- If I typed SKIP at question 8: use style C for everyone.
- If I told you more about my offer: rotate A, B, C, D (1st person A, 2nd B, 3rd C, 4th D, then again).

C - Sample offer (the simple style, 40-70 words)
  Message: "Hi" + first name. One line with their real fact. Then say I have a way [my offer] can help
  [their business] with [a likely problem or benefit], that I have samples of what it looks like,
  and ask if they would be interested in checking them out.
  Example (do not copy word for word): "Hi Dana, I saw Bright Smile just opened a second clinic in Austin.
  I have a way AI videos can help clinics like yours explain treatments before patients even call.
  I have a few samples of what it looks like. Would you be interested in checking them out?"
  Follow-up 1: one short idea of what it could look like for them, plus my samples link (if I gave one).
A - Direct pitch (50-90 words): what my offer is and the result it gets for businesses like theirs. Ask for a quick call.
  Follow-up 1: a concrete example for them, plus my samples link.
B - Idea offer (35-70 words): do not explain the offer. Say I had a specific idea to improve one thing in their
  business and ask: "Would you like me to send it over?" Follow-up 1: the idea in 2-3 sentences, plus my samples link.
D - Problem question (45-80 words): open with a natural question about their likely problem, one line on how my
  offer fixes it, then a soft question. Follow-up 1: a different problem, plus my samples link.
Follow-up 2 for every style (15-35 words): a short, polite last note that is easy to answer yes or no.

Rules for every email:
- Subject: 2-6 words, specific to them, no "Re:", no "!". Different subjects for different people.
- Plain, friendly, simple English. Sounds like one real person writing to one person.
- No links in the first Message. No bold, bullets or emojis.
- NO sign-off and NO name at the end. Just end with the question. My system adds my name.
- Never leave [brackets] or placeholders in an email.
- Never say we have met, spoken or worked together.
- Do not use these words: free, guarantee, risk-free, act now, limited time, click here, urgent.
- Follow-ups are replies in the same email thread: no subject, never "just following up".

=== STEP 6: THE FILE (do NOT change this format) ===
Make a downloadable CSV file named: leads-[who]-[where]-[my first name]-1.csv (no spaces).
The FIRST line must be exactly this, character for character:

Email,First Name,Last Name,Company,Subject,Message,Follow-up 1,Follow-up 2,Angle,Added By,Job Title,Website,Location,Pain Points,Personalization Source,Email Status,Lead Source

Then one line per person:
- Email: lowercase. First Name and Last Name: the person's real names, in separate columns.
- Subject, Message, Follow-up 1, Follow-up 2: the 3 emails.
- Angle: exactly one of "A - Direct pitch", "B - Idea offer", "C - Sample offer", "D - Problem question".
- Added By: my first and last name (answer 2), the same on every line.
- Personalization Source: the link where you found the real fact.
- Email Status: verified, likely, unverified, or "found on website".
- Lead Source: where you found the person, e.g. Apollo, Vibe Prospecting, Google, company website.
- Put EVERY value in double quotes. A double quote inside a value is written twice ("").
- Exactly 17 values per line. An unknown value is "" (never N/A or unknown).

STRICT RULE: a person goes in the file ONLY if Email, First Name, Company, Subject, Message,
Follow-up 1, Follow-up 2 and Angle are all filled in. If not, leave them out. Never guess to fill a gap.

Before you answer, check every line against these rules and fix anything wrong.

=== STEP 7: YOUR ANSWER ===
1. The CSV file to download. If you cannot make files, put the CSV in ONE code block with nothing else in it,
   and say: "Copy this, then paste it into the box in Outreach -> Upload a lead file."
2. One line: "Leads in the file: N. All complete: yes."
3. A short list of people you left out and why (e.g. no email found).
Then stop. When I type MORE: if I gave you my own list, continue with the next people in it;
otherwise find NEW people only. Keep the same style, and number the next file -2, -3 and so on.
