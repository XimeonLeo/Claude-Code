=== STEP 4: PAIN POINTS ===

=== STEP 5: WRITE 3 EMAILS PER LEAD ===
Each lead gets ONE angle for all three emails. Rotate the angles in ANGLES TO TEST (1st lead A, 2nd B, 3rd C...).
Leave out C if SAMPLE WE CAN OFFER is "none", and E if PROOF WE CAN MENTION is "none".
Write the Angle cell exactly as: "A - Direct pitch", "B - Idea offer", "C - Sample offer", "D - Problem question", "E - Proof story".

A - Direct pitch (50-100 words): say plainly what OUR SERVICE is and the result it gets, tied to their main pain point.
    End by asking for CALL TO ACTION. Follow-up 1: a concrete example for them, plus the portfolio link.
B - Idea offer (35-75 words): do NOT explain the service. Say you had a specific idea to improve one thing in their
    business and ask if they would like to see it ("Would you like me to send it over?").
    Follow-up 1: share the idea in 2-3 sentences, plus the portfolio link.
C - Sample offer (45-90 words): offer to make SAMPLE WE CAN OFFER for them at no cost (never say "free"),
    and say in one line why it fits them. Follow-up 1: what their sample would include, plus the portfolio link.
D - Problem question (45-90 words): open with a natural question about their main pain point, one line on why it is
    common, one line on how OUR SERVICE fixes it, then a soft question. Follow-up 1: a different pain point, plus the portfolio link.
E - Proof story (50-100 words): open with PROOF WE CAN MENTION as a short true story, link it to them, end with
    CALL TO ACTION as a question. Follow-up 1: one more detail of the result, plus the portfolio link.
Follow-up 2 for every angle (15-40 words): a short, polite last note that is easy to answer yes or no.

Email rules:
- Subject: 2-6 words, specific to the lead, no "Re:", no exclamation marks. Vary them.
- Message starts with "Hi" + their first name. It uses at least one real fact about their business. No links.
- Follow-up 1 and Follow-up 2 are replies in the same thread: no subject, do not repeat the first email,
  never write "just following up". Use the portfolio link once in Follow-up 1 (only if it is not "none").
- Plain text only. No bold, bullets, emojis.
- NO sign-off and NO name at the end (no "Best regards", no "MY FIRST NAME"). End with the question.
  The system adds the sender's name and an opt-out line.
- Never leave [brackets], {curly braces} or placeholders in an email.
- Never claim we have met, spoken, worked together or been referred.
- Avoid: free, guarantee, risk-free, act now, limited time, 100%, click here, urgent.
- Write in LANGUAGE with TONE. Each email must sound like one real person writing to one person.
